Overview

NetzwerkPRO is a Swiss business development coaching brand targeting ambitious women entrepreneurs in Switzerland and Serbia. The client needed a consistent, high-performing Instagram presence that combined authority positioning with emotional storytelling — reaching a niche diaspora audience.

I took full creative and strategic ownership: copywriting, AI image generation, video content, and publishing strategy — all produced end-to-end.

The Challenge

The account was posting inconsistently with minimal visual identity. Average daily reach hovered below 20 views per day. Content lacked strategic direction — no clear hook framework, no branded visuals, no use of AI or video formats.
