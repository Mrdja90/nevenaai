Problem & Goal

Problem: Kunstzentrum SOKO had an Instagram presence with no visual identity, no content strategy, and no analytics. Posts were published ad-hoc, engagement was unpredictable. Source material consistently consisted of low-quality mobile footage and amateur photography — no professional assets were available.

Goal: Establish a recognizable visual presence and grow audience engagement, within the constraints of a nonprofit budget and limited raw material. Both visual identity and content strategy needed to be built from scratch.

Art Direction

Self-directed creative within nonprofit constraints. No fixed creative brief from the client — direction was largely self-initiated, adapting to each event's tone and available material.

Cultural authenticity meets contemporary aesthetic. Each visual balanced the cultural heritage of the Serbian diaspora community with a clean, modern look to elevate low-quality source material.

Consistent brand identity built over time. A branded AI-generated video outro (produced in Veo and Midjourney) became a recognizable identity marker across all video content — the first consistent brand element SOKO had.

The branded outro — SOKO's golden falcon, now a recognizable closing signature across all video content.

Marketing Applications
Constraint-driven creativity
Consistent on-brand content with no professional assets — directly applicable to startups, nonprofits, or lean teams without photography or video budgets.
Full-funnel content thinking
From awareness (reach beyond follower base) to conversion (profile visits, link clicks, follows) — not just vanity metrics.
AI integration in production
Practical use of AI image and video tools to close the gap between limited resources and professional output.
Analytics as a creative feedback loop
Strategy adjusted based on real performance data — featured performer content, format mix, advance timing — not intuition.
Brand building from zero
Developed a recognizable visual identity for an organization that had none — applicable to any early-stage brand or product launch.

Note on data transparency: Whether individual posts were boosted with paid promotion was not disclosed to me. All metrics reflect account-level reported data. Organic vs. paid breakdown is unknown — results should be interpreted with that caveat in mind.
