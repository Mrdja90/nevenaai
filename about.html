Background

My path to AI creative work is not a straight line — and that's exactly what makes it useful. A chemical engineering degree from the University of Belgrade trained me to think in systems, to hold precision and complexity at the same time. That same rigour carried me into data analysis at Accenture in Warsaw, where I spent over two years handling content review and quality assurance for a Forbes top-10 company — routinely working to accuracy standards above 98%.

While all of that was happening, art was happening too. Group exhibitions. Two published poetry collections. Cover illustrations. These weren't side projects — they were a second education in how meaning travels through an image, and what it takes to stop someone mid-scroll. Running in parallel was a third: teaching myself the AI and digital marketing side, work that led to certifications in Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce and Start Writing Prompts Like a Pro.

"Quality assurance taught me that speed without judgment just produces mistakes faster. AI takes care of the speed, while judgment is still my job."

Since 2025, I've been working as a freelance AI content creator in Basel, Switzerland — offering AI image generation, AI video production, and social media content creation for clients across the country. As a generative AI content creator and a Midjourney and Artlist toolkit artist, I combine hands-on prompt engineering services with the eye of a content strategy AI consultant, shaping AI marketing content that's rooted in genuine brand understanding rather than prompt guesswork. I bring the analytical rigor to measure what works, and the creative instinct to know why.
