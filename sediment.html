SEDIMENT

An editorial study in three states of water.

Water holds, moves and leaves things behind. SEDIMENT follows one woman through three states of it, and through the way experience settles into who we become. Three looks, three abandoned spaces, one face that never changes.

I. Held

Linen cut asymmetric, one shoulder bare, in a derelict swimming pool. Teal tile, rust and a low side light that holds everything still.

II. In motion

Draped copper satin, one shoulder bare, in an abandoned hamam. A dome, an oculus and a single warm shaft of light.

III. Left behind

Muted orchid, sculpted pleats on one side and sheer tulle on the other, on the travertine terraces of Pamukkale at golden hour.

AI-generated. Creative direction, styling, light and motion by Nevena Mučić. One model across ten frames and three films.
