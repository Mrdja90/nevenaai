SEDIMENT — An Editorial Study

SEDIMENT follows water through three states — held, in motion, and left behind — as a metaphor for how experience settles into who we become. Stillness in the pool gives way to movement across ruins and open air, before finally coming to rest in the mineral terraces where the water leaves its mark. Ten frames, one continuous idea: nothing simply happens and disappears — it accumulates.
